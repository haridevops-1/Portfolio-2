My Journey

How I Got Here

Six steps — from electronics to client demos.

Step 1 · The Beginning

Starting with Electronics

I am from Chennai. I did my Diploma in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Central Polytechnic College, Tharamani. This is where my interest in technology began.

Step 2 · Learn & Earn

Working to pay for my learning

I did internships in college and became interested in cloud computing. To pay for my learning, I worked as a Zomato delivery partner for over a year and did part-time jobs. I used my savings to finish my AWS DevOps course.

Step 3 · The Hard Part

20+ rejections, but I kept going

I applied for many jobs and was rejected by more than 20 companies. It was hard, but I did not give up.

Step 4 · The Turning Point

Freshworks STS Software Academy

I heard about Freshworks STS Software Academy and prepared hard. I passed the exam and the interview, and I was selected from more than 2,000 students. There I stayed curious, built technical skills and worked on real projects. I completed the program and received my certificate.

Step 5 · A New Chapter

Joined Spikra as a Product Consultant

I joined Spikra and attended the training program as a Product Consultant. I learned the Zoho products and business strategies.

Step 6 · Today

Moved into Presales

After the training, I moved into Presales. Now I understand client requirements, challenges and pain points, prepare a POC with the Zoho products they need, and demonstrate it.

Presales My role today · still growing
Presales, in simple words

Presales is the work done before a client buys. I am the bridge between the client’s business and the Zoho solution. I learn what the business needs, build a working proof, and show it live — so the client can decide with confidence.

My work, step by step

01

Discovery calls

I talk to the client to understand their business, goals, requirements and pain points.

02

Solution mapping

I match their needs with the right Zoho products.

03

Proof of Concept

I build a working POC based on their requirements, using different Zoho products.

04

Live demo

I demonstrate the POC and show how it solves their problem.

05

Handover

I share the details with my team for the next steps.
